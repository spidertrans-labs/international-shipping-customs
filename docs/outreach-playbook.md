# GitHub Outreach Playbook

## Operating boundary

- Only contact people who publicly discuss a relevant shipping problem, use a related repository, or publish a business contact method.
- Use public business contact details only. Do not scrape private data or use GitHub personal data for bulk email.
- Contact each person at most once. Include the source, relevance, and an opt-out instruction.
- Keep suppression records private and outside this repository.
- Do not post advertisements in Issues or Discussions. Answer the question first and include a service link only when the community permits it and the question asks for a provider.

## Five-hour weekly cadence

- 30 minutes: scan Issues and Discussions for `international shipping`, `cross-border logistics`, `customs clearance`, `集运`, `转运`, and `淘宝集运`.
- 2 hours: write factual answers with official sources where useful.
- 2 hours: verify data, review pull requests, and update high-risk policies.
- 30 minutes: record GitHub traffic, npm downloads, registration-link clicks, and qualified conversations.

## Qualification

A useful lead has at least two signals:

- Public location or stated business market is AU, US, CA, or UK.
- The person discusses importing, consolidation, parcel forwarding, e-commerce fulfillment, or China warehouse needs.
- The person has an active repository, business profile, public website, or recent relevant discussion.
- A public business contact method is available.

Do not add a person to outreach solely because a bio keyword matches. Relevance and an active shipping need are required.

## Weekly targets

- 5 relevant community replies.
- 20 to 30 qualified contacts.
- 1 data or documentation contribution.
- 1 review of traffic, downloads, outbound clicks, and inquiries.
